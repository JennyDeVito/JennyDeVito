### About me

Like most people getting into development today, I use AI to study — but with a clear agreement: the LLM is my professor, not my intern. I'd rather understand how the sausage is made than receive ready-made code I can't explain. I believe understanding the "why" behind each decision is what separates programmers from executors.

Programming languages are one of my most serious hyper-focuses: I want to learn them all. But lately I've been making an effort to improve at the ones I already know.

### Here, you'll find 🔍️

This isn't just a portfolio — it's also my wiki, my configuration backup, and my troubleshooting guide (a love letter to future Jenny).

Here I keep what was developed during college classes, free courses, and bootcamp projects.

Portuguese and English versions aren't always _ipsis litteris_ translations: each language has its own catchphrases, and what matters — the message — is preserved.
