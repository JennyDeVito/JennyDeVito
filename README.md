## A little about me

I use big words - deal with it.

As most of people starting at computer development nowadays I also use IA to study.
But me and ChatGPT have an agreement: it acts as my professor and I don't let it spill the code.
It is very uncomfortable to me just do things without understanding what is happening from behind.

That said, it's important to me to point out that I am strongly against vibe-coding and that is
very hard for me to take vibe coders seriously. It is something that hurts my core beliefs.

Truth is programming language is one of my most serious hyper-focus: I wanna learn them all.
But, lately I've been making an effort to work on improve at the ones I've already know something.

<br>

## Here, you'll find 🔍️

This isn't just a portfolio, is also my wiki, configurations' backup and troubleshooting guide (so to save future Jenny).

Here, I keep what were developed during college classes, free-courses and projetcs made during bootcamps.

Most of my maker projects are on [**WorstMakers**](https://github.com/worstmakers) but some of them are here.

Portuguese and English aren't always _ipsis litteris_ translations: it's not fun to write the same
text in two different languages (it may even take more time!). The catchphrases I use are different
for each language, but the message remains the same.
Don't be a 🤬 and just let me have this.

## Informações importantes

Como a maioria das pessoas hoje em dia eu uso IA para estudar. Mas, eu e o ChatGPT temos um acordo
ele é meu professor e não pode ficar cuspindo código pronto pra mim. Me sinto muito desconfortável em fazer as coisas sem saber como a salsicha é feita.

Isto posto, acredito que é importante pontuar que sou profundamente contra vibe-coding e que, para
mim, é muito difícil levar vibe coders a sério. Pois, é algo que fere meus valores mais básicos.

O negócio é que eu sou seriamente hiper-fixada em linguagens de programação e quero aprender todas.
Mas, ultimamente, estou trabalhando com afinco em aprimorar meus conhecimentos nas linguagens que já conheço.
